Banks won't hedge a $2,000 invoice.
Pooled with others, they will.

Forex hedging has a $50,000 minimum ticket size — not because small exposures are riskier, but because the compliance cost per deal doesn't scale down. FxPool aggregates many small exporters into one bulk forward contract through a GIFT City IFSC banking unit, then splits the outcome back proportionally. Try the flow below — it's the exact mechanism from the research proposal, running live in your browser.

Behind the demo

What's simulated vs. what's real infrastructure

01

Rate calc

Real formula — interest rate differential forward pricing — with assumed sample INR/USD rates. A production build would pull live rates from the IFSC banking partner.

02

Pooling logic

Real bucketing logic — same currency, settlement dates within a window — running client-side against seeded exporters instead of a live order book.

03

OCR intake

Not built here. Production flow would extract amount/currency/due-date from an uploaded invoice automatically.

04

Settlement

Proportional split math is real; actual fund movement always happens through the regulated IFSC banking unit, never through FxPool.
